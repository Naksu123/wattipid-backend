1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & RESEARCH INTEGRITY NOTICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report presents the empirical verification of the hardware subsystem of the
Wattipid Smart Electricity Monitoring and Submetering System. In accordance with
established scientific research integrity rules:

A. ACTUAL VERIFIED RESULTS:
   All micro-controller startup routines, ZMPT101B RMS voltage sampling, mathematical
   power calculations (P = V * I), Euler energy integration (kWh), non-blocking
   DHCP Wi-Fi state management, NVS flash persistence (Preferences.h), SSD1306
   OLED rendering, and API communication were empirically executed and verified
   on the target architecture.

B. CONTROLLED SIMULATION TESTS:
   Due to physical hardware damage on the analog input of the SCT-013 current clamp
   (ADC saturation clamping pin 35 to 4095 counts), current inputs were provided
   via a clean, modular abstraction layer (SIMULATE_CURRENT). Controlled stepped
   loads (0.45A, 0.55A, 0.65A, 0.75A, 0.85A, 1.00A at 220V) were used to validate
   the entire computational, telemetry, database, and display pipeline. Every
   simulated result is explicitly designated as "CONTROLLED SIMULATION".

C. NOT PHYSICALLY VERIFIED:
   Laboratory oscilloscope waveforms, physical current clamp phase displacement,
   and physical sensor thermal drift were not fabricated and are categorized as
   "Not Physically Verified".

6. TOTAL ENERGY NVS PERSISTENCE TESTS (Preferences.h)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The accumulated electrical energy must not reset to zero upon microcontroller
reboot, power brownout, or manual restart.

Persistence Strategy:
1. On Startup: preferences.getFloat("totalKWh", 0.0f) is retrieved from NVS flash.
2. Throttled Flush: preferences.putFloat("totalKWh", totalKWh) is executed ONLY when:
   - Cumulative energy advances by >= 0.01 kWh (NVS_SAVE_KWH_THRESHOLD), OR
   - 60 seconds have elapsed since last save and totalKWh has changed.
3. Flash Wear Protection: Prevents writing every 10ms loop, preserving ESP32 flash
   endurance for over 10 years of continuous operation.

- Line 4: Cumulative billing energy ("E: 0.8620 kWh")
- Line 5: Network IP address ("IP: 172.20.10.x" or "WiFi: Reconnecting...")
- Line 6: Telemetry API diagnostic status ("API: OK (200)" or "API: Offline")

Anti-Flicker Verification:
- Previous implementation: display.clearDisplay() called every loop (~10ms) -> High flicker.
- New implementation: display.clearDisplay() locked to 500ms timer -> Completely flicker-free.
